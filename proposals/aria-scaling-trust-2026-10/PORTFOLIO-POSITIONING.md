# ARIA Scaling Trust portfolio positioning — reviewed 30 October 2026
Sources: https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/building-the-scaling-trust-portfolio/ (7 Oct 2026); https://aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/trust-everything-everywhere/scaling-trust/funded-projects; the 2026 solicitation PDF, Track 2.2.

## Why another funded component is justified
- **University of Edinburgh Agentic Security Reasoner:** derives/formalises secure interaction requirements and evaluates cryptographic protocol properties. ASES proposes to test enforcement of an actual resource/effect boundary **after** a policy/protocol decision exists. No existing integration claimed.
- **Dovetail / Institute for Decentralized AI:** designs verified interaction protocols. ASES focuses on the actual current authorization and complete mediation of selected endpoint effects.
- **ProtoSage / ProtoBench:** agent-based reverse engineering and auditing of protocols. ASES's proposed conformance runner tests concrete host-side authority traces. Audits may be complementary rather than interchangeable.
- **Project HAMMER:** negotiation/bidding safety and incentives under adversarial pressure. ASES tests low-level protected execution and effect revocation.
- **CCTI / Cambridge / SINE:** broader cryptographic trust infrastructure, attestations and portfolio integration. ASES could be a useful enforcement component; engagement is prospective and must not be described as committed.
- **TEE sandboxes and standard OS confinement:** useful isolation primitives and potential realization candidates, but without a verified binding between current authority, already-held routes, recoverable commitments and selected effects they do not by themselves discharge the Work Unit contract. ASES also relies on OS/host guarantees in the current experiments; do not claim superiority before comparative measurement.

## Fit and risk
The October 7 portfolio map explicitly invites proposals for safe bounded access to physical environments, early useful demonstrations and physical evaluations. ASES has *digital* bounded effects/revocation research now, not validated safe actuator control. For Track 2.2, propose a staged digital security primitive and test how it can support later cyber-physical permissions; make any physical demonstration a dependent milestone, not a fact.

Differentiator to defend: **small, declarative authority-to-effect contract plus independent negative controls against real capability escape routes**. Not a new general AI model, not a negotiation protocol, not a complete hypervisor, not an all-purpose cryptographic proof.

Potential weakness: ARIA prioritizes tools usable by all Arena participants and stronger early multi-agent demonstration, while current F3 is single-host/single-child. Address via explicit future adapter and two-principal demo milestones, not invented present coverage. The applicant's overseas location/UK net benefit and named technical team are also substantive evaluation risks.

Track decision: Primary draft **2.2 Components**, consistent with existing preference. **3.1 Formal AI Security** could fit the theory work but is a different proposal requiring distinct scope and should not be submitted as a copy-paste variant; ARIA requires separate proposals for different tracks.
