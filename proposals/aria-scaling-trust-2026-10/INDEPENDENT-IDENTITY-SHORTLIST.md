## Decision criteria
Distinct from the existing business and ASES tool names; credible in research papers and ARIA review; pronounceable and memorable; not solely AI-branded; supports future security, distributed systems and formal-methods research; legal/trademark distinguishability; domain and GitHub/social consistency; acquisition cost low and manageable as an independent individual.

## Acquisition sequence (after applicant selects a candidate)
1. Search registered legal names and UK/US/EU trademark databases and conflicts in software/security/research classes. A web query does not replace a professional trademark review.
2. Check **live registrar/RDAP** availability of the desired \`.org\` or \`.research\` and a reasonable fallback \`.com\`; confirm exact renewal costs and registrant privacy. Do not treat lack of indexed search results as an available domain.
3. Verify proposed GitHub organization handle, matching public code-host mirror if desired, and Bluesky/Mastodon/social handles using their actual signup/availability systems. No handles were checked or reserved during this planning pass.
4. Reserve the chosen domain and organization **only with explicit applicant authorization**. Store credentials in applicant-owned secret stores, keep administrative ownership distinct from the business, and create independent public-facing contact details.
5. Publish one concise project landing page and link pinned older ASES evidence with attribution and exact SHA; plan source migration separately using verified preserved mirrors. Do not move original repos or claim rights before legal/provenance checks.
6. Update grant PDF, commercial hypothesis and portal entries only after identity is truly secured.
