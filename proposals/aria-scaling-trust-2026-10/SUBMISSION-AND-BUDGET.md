## Applicant structure — confirmed 30 October 2026
- **Applicant/PI:** one **unhosted individual**, using their own legal name and identity as the contracting/funded party; legal name and tax residence still to be provided privately.
- **Rock Solid Sites:** established public GitHub/project identity only. It is not incorporated or registered as a usable grant applicant, and is not to be represented as a legal company, employer, host organization or formal co-applicant.
- **Eligibility:** ARIA's Scaling Trust FAQ says it can fund unhosted individuals without a limited company or host organization; all awardees undergo due diligence. If the award involves contractors/collaborators, ARIA suggests considering sole-trader/company structure and independent tax advice **if selected**. This does not prevent applying now as an individual.
- **Team:** one human PI; additional technical/security experts are proposed contractors or recruits, not confirmed personnel. ARIA says AI agents may be listed as team members if their status is explicit. Our default narrative describes them as tools/contributors under the PI, not as human employees.
- **Remaining private applicant fields:** legal name, residence/tax details, actual FTE and bio, legal title/ownership of project background IP, and consent to grant conditions. Do not invent or store identifying information in this public repository.
- **Source:** https://aria.org.uk/media/1lcjzczg/scaling-trust-faqs-webinar-1.pdf (Team Composition + Solo Applicants, pp. 5–6).

## Nonnegotiable submission format
- Portal account and submission link through official funding page; confirm current fields in live portal before pasting.
- Proposal PDF <=10 pages including diagrams, references excluded; Arial at least 11pt, black, single-spaced, margin >=0.5 in. Summary <=500 words (February solicitation PDF pp. 27–30; live page supersedes its obsolete March deadline).
- Track 2.2 chosen in primary proposal. For other tracks ARIA requests separate proposals.
- Team and applicant details, commitment FTE and start/hiring timeline. Lead/key researchers generally 50%+ and preferably 80% time per old PDF.
- Budget in ARIA's downloadable **official spreadsheet**, not only text. ARIA's eligible cost guidance governs; monthly breakdown expected pre-contract, applicant can submit yearly/quarterly/monthly phasing.
- Separate **commercial hypothesis upload** using ARIA's guidelines/template (not a substitute in proposal PDF).
- Portal administrative responses: individual award/contact/eligibility details, amount/cofunding, existing/background IP and rights, concurrent/related grants, conflicts/restrictions, commercialization, majority outside UK and UK contribution, and acceptance of individual award and due-diligence terms.
- Funded foreground software MIT + Apache-2.0 dual license; background rights and third-party dependencies separately audited. Applicant outside UK must detail credible UK program benefit and activities; do not claim majority UK project spend/people unless true.
- UK build weeks/travel costs should be included, matching actual plans. No fabricated UK collaborators.
- Review criteria: worth shooting for, differentiated, well defined/rapid execution, responsible, intrinsic motivation/team, and benefit to the UK.

1. Individual's legal name and country of tax residence, rights to the ASES background work, personal grant eligibility and authorization. **Individual status is confirmed; legal name is not.**
2. Applicant and proposed team short bios, time fractions, available or proposed hires, and expertise gaps; no false human specialist claims.
3. Preferred amount and project duration, actual personnel and budget costs, current funding, and whether the team can begin promptly.
4. Specific credible UK benefit: UK travel duration and participation only when personally confirmed, UK collaborations only when agreed, expected UK economic/scientific impact and costs.
5. Background IP/license eligibility and any potential conflicts or restrictions.
6. Named commercial hypothesis / intended users, without invented customer traction.
