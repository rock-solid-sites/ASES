## Source-first P5 reconstruction — distinct from independent agent review

The P7 verifier reopened both previously archived original P5 red/green GitLab ZIPs, verified their file sizes and SHA-256, checked ZIP CRC, parsed their original `results/p5.json`, and independently **recomputed assertion outcomes from expected/observed pairs** rather than trusting stored `PASS` or `FAIL` flags.

The recomputed finding agreed with P5's original raw observations:

| P5 candidate, in both Rust and Haskell | Conventional restart checks | Conventional plus checkpoint-qualified SIGKILL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct durability/idempotency | No violation observed in selected checks | No violation observed in selected checks |
| Duplicate-ID fault | Detected | Detected |
| ACK before append/fsync | Missed | Detected at ACK checkpoint, five repetitions per language |

P7 explicitly counted failed assertions in *planted faulty candidates* separately from unexpected failures of correct candidates. It also verified each recorded checkpoint marker, `-9` child exit, recovered journal state, retry result and five-repetition grouping from raw stored fields. This is **programmatic independent recomputation in a new harness**, **not a different-model independent reviewer**.

The **initial failed P5** remained failed in the source record. A direct pinned-source comparison of P5 harness revisions `b20bc9dc7829ae7f4c04fe718c371b12ad958fea` and `0e25c4b8970a0064af4d4169f68f5ac7e0cfab2e` found **exactly one changed line**: an alleged over-cap fixture changed from `charge=50, cap=80` to `charge=90, cap=80`. Fifty does not exceed eighty, whereas ninety does; this establishes that the original unexpected assertion was a **test-oracle mistake**, not evidence of a solver defect. Original raw outcome and later interpretation are separate records.

## Ontology-review candidate benchmark — only baseline qualified

The author created six fixed-scope cases across distinct roles: three Design and three Conformance, with **four seeded defects and two clean controls** (one clean in each role). Frozen labels and intended counterexamples are in the private key.

A deliberately inexpensive **syntax-only deterministic control** flagged **2/4** planted defects, missed **2/4**, and raised **0/2** false positives on clean cases. The baseline did not analyze proposed conceptual designs comprehensively and is not a substitute for an ordinary language-model reviewer. The P7 source packet validator also rejected **7/7 deliberately malformed or contaminated case packets**.

This is useful for quantifying why pure lexical/syntax guards are not sufficient on every conceptual task, but **cannot** demonstrate that either proposed ontology role adds value. Actual ordinary-review, Ontology Design Reviewer and Ontology Conformance Reviewer performances, model families, costs, contamination rates and disagreements are all **NOT_RUN / NOT_OBSERVED**.

## Post-hoc discovery: the first Conformance packet leaked result hints

After the initial P7 measurement, inspection of the original corpus showed that some Conformance candidate objects included `observed_trace` or `relevant_trace` explaining the anticipated outcome. Although these are not answer-key labels, they would bias an actual blind conformance review.

The original source, scoring key and P7 outcome were preserved unchanged. A **new** [Conformance Review packet v2](../ontology-benchmark-p7/conformance-review-packet-v2.json) removed those traces while preserving the frozen definitions and exact finished artifact excerpts. The separate [Design Review packet](../ontology-benchmark-p7/design-review-packet.json) retains its permitted domain/design rationale. The initial [Conformance Review packet v1](../ontology-benchmark-p7/conformance-review-packet.json) is historical and **must not be dispatched** for a blind evaluation.

A **post-hoc, not pre-registered** packet-qualification test was then run:

- [GitLab pipeline 3016118845](https://gitlab.com/rock-solid-sites-group/rock-solid-sites-project/-/pipelines/3016118845), job `17652511333`, **SUCCESS**.
- [Versioned packet-integrity checker](../ontology-benchmark-p7/qualify_role_packets.py) accepted **2/2** valid role-specific packets, detected **6/6** deliberate contamination, cross-role, source-modification and missing-invariant injections.
- Instrument runtime **0.450 ms**; full CI job **24.545 seconds**.
- [Complete original post-hoc CI artifact ZIP](../ontology-benchmark-p7/evidence/p7-cleanroom-packet-qualification-original-ci.zip.b64) is retained independently of GitLab; [SHA-256 verified provenance](../ontology-benchmark-p7/evidence/p7-cleanroom-packet-archive-manifest.json), original ZIP **1,823 bytes** and SHA-256 `741c9773aec3724ef15202d9a103a77ccc655bb51fa57dc324c7d4063edf252b`.

This correction is an example of **instrument qualification discovering a problem in the evaluation process itself**; it does not retroactively validate the original v1 packet as blind.

## Method evaluation, scope and next decision

P7 supports: honest preservation of a failed CI job and later correction; reproduction of P5's conclusions from original per-check evidence; source-level confirmation of the single erroneous fixture change; bounded packet validation and detection of a review-context leak; low-cost deterministic baseline comparison on six selected cases.

P7 does **not** support: claims that an independent reviewer has examined P5; claims that the two proposed ontology reviewer roles outperform ordinary review; unbiased defect-discovery rate; confirmed different-model-family isolation; authorizing a new standard testing gate. A reviewer packet and source-first replay are not substitutes for those missing tests.

[Review dispatch/measurement protocol](P7-REVIEW-EXECUTION-PROTOCOL-2026-11-02.md) specifies three **future** isolated routes—ordinary review, Ontology Design and Ontology Conformance—using the corrected inputs and private key only after frozen outputs. The formal reviewer comparison is **NOT_RUN** until actual model routes with caller-side identity/provenance and bounded costs exist.

**Decision:** retain the P7 evidence and corrected benchmark as experimental. Methodology and reviewer-role adoption **DEFERRED**; original PR #4, canonical methods, production Resource Solver and Trip'n CI monitor unchanged.
