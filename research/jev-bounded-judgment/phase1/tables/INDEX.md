## Standing caveats

- n=64 synthetic cases. No significance test is claimed anywhere.
- The `rule` and `lexical` cue lexicons were hand-authored with the corpus vocabulary in view: they are hand-built baselines, and their accuracy is an upper bound on a generic cue engine.
- `rule` on area D evaluates the same policy that defines the expected role, so its 12/12 is tautological.
- Jev reports no confidence for `noul` questions, which is 50 of 64 cases; escalation coverage therefore rests on a locally derived value.
- `general_model` is hard-decoded, so its confidence is identically 1.0 and its threshold coverage is an encoding artefact.
