# Stale disposition decision across recovery — preregistration

The inherited F1 remove guard checks its finite Work Unit descendants and sealed status, but does not represent all durable content or partially completed disposition. Closure H13/H14 requires a complete current dependency domain, including unaccepted durable candidates; C5/C8 require surviving dependents to remain bounded across loss and before final removal. A stale earlier empty-inventory check is not evidence of present emptiness. This test uses sealed leaf S; the candidate z is an abstract unaccepted durable object whose containment depends on S, not a new Work Unit descendant. Its creation after a check is stipulated as an independently authorized internal effect despite sealing; no claim is made that sealing stops internal computation.

Pre-run hypothesis: an earlier prepare observes zero dependents, then an authorized internal effect commits z inside S; F1 stop and recovery occur; a current complete disposition check must refuse final S removal. A mutant that relies on the saved earlier empty observation instead permits the inherited F1 remove, leaving z without its required boundary; a separate observer detects that violation. A positive case with no intervening z must remove S successfully under both checks. A second positive control after recovery explicitly transfers z to already bounded Q in an authorized whole abstract transition, then current checking must permit S removal and show z remains bounded by Q. `prepare` and `create z` are separate serialized abstract transitions, so this is an explicit check/use interleaving with process loss; stop/recovery do not themselves erase the candidate or stale preparation. Commitment-time check and removal are assumed one atomic serialized step, not demonstrated host enforcement. Current full inventory, trusted ownership and transfer authorization are premises.

Use the inherited F1 transition guard/invariant and sequence: seal S, prepare, optional create z, stop, recover_start, recover_step P/S, activate_image, attempt remove S, optional transfer z then remove. Trace both a reachable unsafe mutated commit and non-vacuous correct removal. The dependency and complete inventory are abstract additions absent from inherited F1, not evidence of a real implementation or an exhaustive disposition system.
