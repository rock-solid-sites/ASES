# Exact authority comparison at two-store crash cuts — preregistration

After observing the weaker eligibility rule admit old at the record-first cut in `Two-Store-Crash-Cuts.md`, this separately preregistered follow-up tests a directly available alternative. Reuse that experiment's actual SQLite image/store constructors and writer, with the same five independent process-loss cuts and fresh readers. A reader admits a candidate only if integrity passes, its exact epoch and digest equal both the recovered authoritative record and the recovered witness. The independent parent checks admitted epoch/digest against the known expected current candidate and the appropriate cut's expected authority state; the parent does not participate in admission. This is a test of exact agreement, not proof that the authority or witness is non-rollback or independently trustworthy.

Pre-run hypothesis: both middle cuts reject both candidates because authority and witness differ; initial admits exact old; both completed orders admit exact new. As a reachable negative control in each run, the non-current candidate must refuse. All five writers must be killed/reaped after the selected committed cut, all four SQLite files pass integrity in fresh readers, and all candidate decisions and recovered values must be recorded. Five deterministic histories, no enumeration, 30-second timeout. Premises and exclusions from `Two-Store-Crash-Cuts.md` remain, including process-only crash, already trusted and current authority record and witness, no concurrent updates, storage corruption or adversarial rollback, no failures during commit or host power loss. Exact agreement introduces availability loss during disagreement and does not atomically publish the two records, determine which is right, or repair the gap. Partial C7/C12 and Work Unit E/F only; F1/F5 remain open.
