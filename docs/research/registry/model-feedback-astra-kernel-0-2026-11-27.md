# Astra model feedback — November 2026 foundational research

## Identification and scope

Operator-selected OpenAI Astra in the ASES cloud research environment; exact served model identifier, release/build, effective reasoning configuration and token accounting not independently attested. Operator reports no subagents in the two final autonomous research runs. This is an evidence entry under the existing registry, not a routing rule or a relative model ranking.

The task was open-ended research into kernel and Work Unit adequacy, with the established three primitives as working design hypotheses and access to accumulated research. Successive substantive findings were to be preserved rather than treating the first successful investigation as completion. Earlier clean-room derivations are a different task/context and not directly comparable.

## Observed capabilities

| Dimension | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| Foundational reasoning | Derived conditional indistinguishability/state lower bounds for tenure reuse and online recovery stamp maintenance; distinguished one-shot validation from the state necessary to maintain its answer. |
| Compositional reasoning | Derived a common-cut test for one handover, an arbitrary-member induction for repeated replacements, and extensions concerning abort, membership and replay of escaped votes. |
| Complexity and verification strategy | Constructed an NP-completeness reduction for policy-preserving relocations and a tractable restricted class; characterized sufficient revalidation observations via distinguishability under interference. |
| Experiment construction | Produced bounded schedule/decision-table searches, negative controls, symbolic obligations, and a three-SQLite-store accepted-prefix transfer experiment covering ten process-loss cuts. |
| Autonomous research and durability | Successive research milestones retained and indexed on main; final report identifies native-owner correspondence as an open obligation, not a demonstrated property. |

The [pinned research index](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/blob/ed0266c4a9e606a2b5112cc0aeaa19221561dea2/docs/research/kernel-0/README.md) and [commit](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/commit/ed0266c4a9e606a2b5112cc0aeaa19221561dea2) supply the source record. The run reports successful replay of all 25 Python scripts and agreement of Z3 4.13.3 and cvc5 1.4.2 on six SMT-LIB files (31 unsatisfiable proof obligations, 12 satisfiable negative controls per solver). The index distinguishes same-host replay from independent model authorship and engine conformance. The coordinating Chat read the index and selected reports; it did not independently rerun the final suite or verify every proof. These are substantial scoped research artifacts, not a general adequacy proof.

## Operational observations and uncertainty

An earlier run concluded after its first verified finding. The subsequent extended brief treated findings as milestones and produced a broader body of research. This suggests a completion-condition effect, not a controlled causal comparison. The final runs were interrupted by operator-reported allowance exhaustion. Detailed account-specific usage and recovery checkpoints remain in operational records, not this public entry. Exact token consumption, relative efficiency, latency distribution and resource attribution are unmeasured here. Do not infer a fixed runtime limit, cost or comparative superiority.

Evidence does not establish native engine correspondence, physical power-loss durability, general kernel adequacy/irreducibility, or the general superiority of Astra over Sol, full GLM-5.3, or other models. No matched autonomous research comparison has yet established those capability gaps. Distinguish exact models, harnesses, providers, configurations and subscriptions when interpreting future observations. Where judgment is necessary, anonymize outputs where practical and include cross-family criticism; independently verify claims instead of treating reviewer preferences as proof.
