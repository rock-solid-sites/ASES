## Decision and stopping rule

Frozen retention rule: >=2 net additional complete supported answers without added unsupported/authority errors, or comparable correctness and >=25% fewer source reads. Strict delta -1, relaxed delta -1, candidate count delta 0. No new complete answer relative to baseline and no reading-count gain. Raw provenance is wrong in every selected index occurrence. Thus no demonstrated marginal retrieval value for this corpus, and known identity defects would need correction if a later separately justified use established benefit. DISCARD for this scoped use, retain source-pinned artifacts, known failure modes and reusable lessons; ordinary repository search is adequate here. Not a claim that FTS5 or graph indexing is universally useless. Do not repair or expand the index, alter active skills, merge branches, add provider or vector ingestion, deploy service, or open a fresh experiment from these findings. Owner review can check the receipt and grading; parent ASES-19 retains human decision authority.

The operational admission pilot also incurred preparation/dispatch overhead (two bounded independent review attempts, one complete; 11,752 completion tokens, provider-reported cash $0). The benchmark itself used no inference. This illustrates the need for cheap deterministic source qualification and reusable review infrastructure; it does not establish net savings or a universal mandatory gate. ASES-28 methodology is separate and not promoted globally.
