# Independent foundational research pilot

Status: experimental research assignment; not an architectural decision.

## Assignment

Conduct an autonomous research programme advancing the foundational design, adequacy and verification of ASES.

Treat ISOLATE, CONNECT and ORDER and the existing Work Unit architecture as working design hypotheses. Use the accumulated public ASES research, historical reasoning, adversarial reviews and experimental evidence, beginning with the [Kernel-0 research index at ed0266c](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/blob/ed0266c4a9e606a2b5112cc0aeaa19221561dea2/docs/research/kernel-0/README.md). Consult relevant external research freely.

Independently identify consequential unresolved questions and pursue those offering the greatest potential research advances. Develop substantive arguments, proofs, counterexamples, experiments or reusable research techniques rather than merely plans or summaries. Challenge existing conclusions where evidence warrants it.

Treat verified findings as milestones. Preserve them, reassess the remaining research frontier and proceed to another valuable investigation while the authorized run continues. Route around individual blockers where possible. Work independently without subagents.

Use current ASES conventions. Record the starting revision and preserve recoverable checkpoints frequently. Publish useful results as explicitly experimental research under `docs/research/kernel-0/glm-g4/` on main; keep the prior research intact. Distinguish demonstrated results, conditional arguments, hypotheses and remaining proof obligations. Keep raw session records, credentials and private material out of public artifacts. No production adoption or deployment.

The operator controls the execution boundary; do not choose or substitute models, launch other agents, or attempt to inspect credentials or expand authorization.
