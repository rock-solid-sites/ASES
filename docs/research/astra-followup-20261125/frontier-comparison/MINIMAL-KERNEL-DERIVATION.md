# Participant prompt

Derive the smallest defensible custom Kernel-0 for ASES from the established requirements:

https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/blob/a02df749b4b3da88d3f88c1712623cec6ad5d963/docs/research/kernel-0/Kernel-0-Evidence-Packet.md

Reason independently from first principles, without consulting previous designs, implementation experiments or other reasoning sessions.

Go beyond restating requirements:

- Derive a candidate irreducible ontology, primitive vocabulary and transition semantics.
- Demonstrate how the primitives compose to satisfy the Work Unit architecture and its authority, continuity and concurrency requirements.
- Attempt to eliminate, combine or derive each proposed primitive. Identify concrete counterexamples where a weaker substrate fails.
- Examine indirect authority, composition, revocation, conflicting changes, recovery and unresolved external effects.
- Distinguish demonstrated necessities from sufficient mechanisms, policy choices and assumptions. Identify any requirements that cannot be satisfied simultaneously.

Do not disguise complexity inside an unrestricted general-purpose primitive or an unspecified trusted mechanism.

Return the strongest minimal candidate you can justify, its limitations, competing interpretations and the most consequential remaining research questions.

This is a bounded independent reasoning task. Do not implement or delegate.
