## Disposition rules and limitations

- Retain explicit deferrals and supersessions rather than automatically reopening them.
- Deduplicate research questions, not merely names or technical mechanisms; shared evidence does not imply identical objectives.
- A named donor, suggestion or historical link is not an accepted work item.
- Treat unavailable historical artifacts as a custody gap, not permanent loss or proof no work happened.
- The register is reconciled against inspected evidence and current owner records, not a complete search of every later conversation, branch or local session. Mechanical screening coverage and semantic review coverage remain separate in [COVERAGE](COVERAGE.md).
- No work dispatch, experimental execution, policy change, new project, issue creation, or source archive mutation was performed. Exact source locators and restricted dispositions remain in the private execution record.
