# First synthesis

This is a navigation and reconciliation result, not a new research taxonomy or execution plan. The [register](IDEAS.md) and [reconciliation](RECONCILIATION.md) distinguish known evidence, outstanding questions and historical decisions. The complete candidate disposition ledger and cross-portfolio subject organization remain in the authorized private execution record.

## Recurring distinctions that change the next investigation

**1. Recover evidence before recreating experiments.** The Jev record (HIR-010) illustrates a custody problem: surviving experimental design and partial results, later reports, and incomplete access to measured artifacts. Another run would not recover the original measurement or establish the lineage of the existing evidence. The operator retains the conclusions despite possible raw-artifact loss. Preserve the original limitations; recover precise artifacts if available and useful, without treating recovery or replication as a prerequisite.

**2. A verified instrument is not a verified research result.** P7 (HIR-023) has a prepared corpus and a deterministic control, not measured independent review. Experiment-admission research (HIR-006) has a pilot result, not a proven universal admission gate. The claim-driven target study (HIR-015) is not resolved by method testing on another target. Preserve these different evaluation objects and compare like with like.

**3. Distinguish the mechanism from its use and its value.** The knowledge-index prototype (HIR-009) and the identifier-first experiments (HIR-035) provide implementation evidence within bounded scopes. They do not answer every proposed comparative evaluation, establish production authorization, or justify starting another implementation. Retain the owner and formulate the residual question precisely.

**4. Composition and coordination are not the same as local correctness.** Current Kernel-0 evidence is bounded by explicit assumptions (HIR-022/031); it does not establish general software integration validity. The Git engineering questions (HIR-008) concern interruption, recovery, intervention and evidence freshness under actual development constraints. Preserve the difference between formal substrate guarantees and observed project-level outcomes.

**5. Current decisions can resolve a historical implementation without resolving its motivation.** The task-scoped method and superseded automatic export design (HIR-005/007) should not be reopened as competing architecture. Useful evidence preservation, actual behavioral effects and recovery remain separately investigable where they are not already covered. Deliberate deferral is a recorded decision, not accidental omission.

**6. Measurement has its own object and cost.** Capability evidence, resource allocation and actual execution measurements (HIR-030) cannot substitute for one another. Reviews, model capability observations, queue or provider state, and validated results represent different kinds of evidence. Instrumentation costs and missing or censored observations affect comparisons; do not make an observation mechanism mandatory solely because it was proposed.

## Follow-up order

Evidence-resolution order, not project priority: retain historical conclusions with their custody limitations, reconcile benchmark identities and review lineage, and establish specific residual questions with existing owners. Any later decision to run a study should identify the evidence that would discriminate alternatives, why existing results do not suffice, and its proportional cost. Reuse the existing experiment-admission procedure and related research; this synthesis does not authorize experiments.
