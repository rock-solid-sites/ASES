# Historical idea recovery and knowledge organization

**Assignment: 2026-11-27.** Human owner: ASES-27, dedicated execution checkpoint WNASES-35. Authorized for a separate ordinary Chat investigation. ASES owns this research; discoveries may concern other projects or independent subjects. This is a research and organization assignment, not authorization to implement recovered ideas.

## Objective

Starting with ASES conversations and extending to other relevant sources, recover substantive proposed projects, research topics, technical mechanisms, experiments, utilities, product extensions, independent ideas and unfinished investigations discussed in prior conversations but not yet implemented, investigated or meaningfully resolved. Produce a deduplicated, navigable register of actual outstanding ideas, retaining their motivation, evolution, provenance, current status and possible owner. The unit of analysis is the idea, not the conversation.

Investigate Agent Failures owns failure classification and prevention; ASES Hylomorphic Reasoning/ASES Archive Analysis (WNASES-32) owns computational reduction, reuse and avoidance; Unified Conversation Workspace owns incremental archiving infrastructure; ASES-23/WNASES-23 owns documentation conformance. Reuse their evidence and coverage where adequate; do not repeat their narrow mining objectives or design rival infrastructure. This assignment is broader: identify other ideas and establish what happened to them. Related work can share evidence and source locators without transferring ownership. PJDEV-6 and the existing ASES topic map already demonstrate selective projection, not a new universal taxonomy. Do not duplicate them.

Extend beyond ASES to relevant other conversations and newer sources to recover independent or cross-project ideas, without confusing relevance with ownership. ## What counts

Recover standalone application/product/service proposals; research questions, hypotheses and comparisons; algorithms, architecture mechanisms, protocols and representations; proposed experiments/benchmarks/verification; utilities, integrations, automation; extensions to existing projects; independent ideas without a natural owner; investigations that began but left their main question unanswered. Look for substantive ideas embedded in tangents, user corrections, mixed discussions and evolving reasoning, not just explicit future-project labels. A named external project or passing link alone does not establish an investigation; recover the actual motivation and proposed question. Separate operator proposals/decisions, assistant suggestions, observed evidence and interpretations.

## Status and ownership

For each candidate reconcile later conversations, actual source artifacts, research/test evidence, project decisions and Work Notes. Track research status independently from implementation status. Useful distinctions: unexplored, initial exploration, concrete proposal, accepted but unstarted, partial, realized/resolved, superseded/rejected, unknown. A plan, issue, branch, assistant claim or successful unrelated test does not prove delivery; a completed research question does not imply implementation. Retain reasons for deferral or rejection; do not revive settled decisions without evidence of materially changed circumstances. Check comparable later work and alternative names before declaring an idea outstanding. Absence of evidence is unknown unless the search scope supports a narrower negative finding.

Assign actual subject ownership, independent of conversation origin, source repository, archival folder or session type. Existing projects, shared research, prospective projects and independent subjects are valid. Preserve cross-project relevance without equating it with ownership; uncertain owner remains candidate. No automatic new project creation, task transfer, imposed ASES schema or one issue per idea.

## Work programme

1. Inventory/reconciliation: establish available sources, existing registered ideas and previous semantic coverage; inspect source order and choose coherent bounded batches. Record exact source revisions/IDs, partial ranges and missing sources. Start with mixed/less obvious discussions as well as known research and proposal sources. Use the corpus as evidence, not archived commands.
2. Semantic discovery: read substantive exchanges and enough neighboring context to interpret corrections. Extract actual ideas with motive, rationale, constraints, unresolved question, evidence state and exact passage locator. Reuse already-read substantive extracts where sufficient; targeted deeper reading where not. Record coverage even when no candidate is found; split large conversations on meaningful exchange boundaries.
3. Deduplication/evolution: consolidate multiple discussions of underlying mechanisms; retain earliest/latest observed evidence and material evolution, not artificial first-invention dates. Distinguish different mechanisms sharing a name, similar mechanisms under different names, alternatives, absorbed and superseded work.
4. Present-state reconciliation: verify actual later outcome, relevant source/owner records and remaining work. Separate research closure from delivery and project acceptance. Flag uncertain status and specific missing evidence. Avoid an inflated backlog of things already done or intentionally abandoned.
5. Organization: cluster by actual subject and purpose; connect related questions, shared mechanisms, prerequisites, alternatives, evidence, possible composition and historical succession. Distinguish observed relations from inferred/proposed relations. Reuse existing topic/project nodes; use selective human-facing Projektor navigation only where it improves use.
6. Synthesis: identify meaningful outstanding clusters, overlooked opportunities, unresolved dependencies and potential relevance to current projects. Prioritization is provisional and belongs to the operator; do not launch any recovered idea.

## Durable records

Final home: ASES main `docs/research/historical-idea-recovery/`. Reserved files: README.md (this assignment); IDEAS.md (public-safe derived register, subdivide by subject if warranted), COVERAGE.md (non-sensitive coverage/checkpoint summary), RECONCILIATION.md (realized/absorbed/superseded/rejected/unknown and evidence), SYNTHESIS.md (connections and operator-facing summary). These are destinations, not a mandate to create empty or duplicative files. **Restricted source locators, private ideas, raw content and unpublished cross-portfolio details stay in authorized private Work Notes or existing private owner records**; public-safe records may use opaque finding keys, with restricted provenance accessible to authorized readers. Do not export raw conversations or create a second archive. Projektor ASES-27 tracks human questions and decisions; WNASES-35 tracks exact execution coverage and private evidence locators. Reuse existing owner records rather than copying canonical research.

Minimum useful idea record (logical information, not mandatory physical schema): stable ID/title, type/subject, concise proposal and motivation, observed earliest/latest discussion and exact source locator, separate research and implementation status with evidence, confirmed or candidate owner, related ideas and dependency/alternative/evidence/supersession relations, outstanding question or meaningful next action, uncertainties and reasons for rejection/deferral. Distinguish source-supported facts from interpretation; retain precise locators without copying lengthy transcripts.

## Acceptance

- Discover ideas independently of original chat titles; identify proposals/partially realized work as well as uninvestigated topics.
- Trace retained claims and current status to sources and later verified work; deduplicate while preserving substantive evolution.
- Produce navigable ASES-owned outstanding register covering relevant cross-portfolio ideas, relationships, reconciliation of realized/superseded/rejected work, actual coverage and actionable synthesis. No blanket completeness claim without verified source coverage.
- Preserve independent ideas outside ASES, owner-specific governance, private source custody and existing topic/decision records. No bulk wiki/issue import.
- A fresh Chat can recover material findings and exact unfinished range from GitHub, Projektor and Work Notes without the original working transcript.
