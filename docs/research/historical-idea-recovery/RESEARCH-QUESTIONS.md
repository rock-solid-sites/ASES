## Method efficacy — separate process compliance from outcomes

The [Phase 4 retrospective](../retrospectives/phases/Phase%204.md) preserves the original evaluation ambition, including safety and effective engineering for nonprogrammers, different model capabilities, speed and token expenditure. The [Evaluation Framework](../frameworks/Evaluation%20Framework.md) distinguishes implementation fidelity, methodology outcomes, research quality and experimental comparisons. **Residual question:** on a bounded, comparable engineering task, does a specified methodological intervention improve externally verified outcomes, rework, elapsed time, model/resource expenditure, recovery and operator intervention? A component test establishes a component claim, not an overall methodology advantage; adherence to the process is not an independent success criterion. Reuse the existing evaluation framework and specific experimental work; no new permanent benchmark or mandatory gate follows from this recovery.

## Further empirical questions within existing research (R20–R22)

These are residual questions, not adopted rules or independent work packages. The historical [methodology feedback/enforcement design](../EDASES-Methodology-Feedback-and-Enforcement.md) is experimental; the current [methodology-to-requirements mapping](../../requirements/Methodology%20to%20Requirements%20Mapping%20Specification.md) distinguishes applicable, authorized, mechanically expressible rules from universal enforcement. Compare advice, warning and hard enforcement for a particular eligible rule against an ungated baseline; measure false rejection/acceptance, exceptions, overhead and avoided rework. Evidence for adopting a rule is distinct from implementation conformance.

The existing [task-execution evidence and MQI research](../Task%20Execution%20Evidence%20and%20Model%20Quality%20Index%20Integration%202026-11-01.md) and resource solver already address forecasting and calibration. The historical project-level question is narrower: compare actual comparable delivery outcomes with original forecasts, separating elapsed time, active operator time, agent work, concurrency, scope changes, failed attempts, coordination and rework. A single successful project or token count cannot establish estimation bias or the cost of a project.

Two related methodology-efficacy refinements are (i) minimum effective role structure, comparing independence, defect coverage, recovery and coordination cost rather than assuming emergent roles are optimal, and (ii) independent review timing, comparing ordinary checks with bounded targeted review at appropriate stages. The preserved historical [precommit checklist skill](../../historical/harness-setup-2026/claude-source/skills/review-pre-commit/SKILL.md.txt) demonstrates a documented checklist, not independent model invocation or measured benefit. No new role ontology or blanket review gate follows.

For agent-oriented framework choice, a concrete matched comparator is identical design tasks in plain HTML/CSS, Astro, React and a current agent-oriented WordPress workflow. Distinguish representational legibility, constraint quality and prior familiarity; compare fidelity, defects, editing and rework, elapsed time and model/resource use. Historical migration results motivate, but do not control, such a comparison. Do not reopen settled framework choices absent a live question.

The surviving [Efficiency Architecture research branch](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/blob/codex/efficiency-architecture-research/docs/architecture/EDASES-Efficiency-Architecture.md) and [discriminating experiment inventory](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/blob/codex/efficiency-architecture-research/docs/architecture/efficiency/Efficiency-Experiments.md) were read back (blobs `d5ff094e9c8faace2b98a145f4d86bedbfd2839c` and `de707d32d12a50f5d4b3ec25b7197189a4b70f35`). ## R23 — human-interface research and artifact sharing

- **Capability onboarding:** distinguish visualization of execution from learning to exercise useful capabilities. A meaningful real project, optional context-sensitive guidance and a small action vocabulary are historical proposals, not a validated learning intervention. Compare task success, discovery, later transfer and operator control with ordinary documentation; do not tie permissions to inferred competence. Existing UI synthesis is a representation study, not evidence of tutorial efficacy.
- **Shareable workflow safety:** distinguish outbound approval from the safety of receiving, inspecting, importing and deliberately executing a shared artifact. A provenance-preserving exchange must not carry its creator's credentials, grants or other authority into a recipient's environment. Existing prompt-injection fixtures address narrower content boundaries; a complete exchange guarantee has not been established.
- **Distributed-systems lineage:** an earlier explicit deferred comparison of established distributed mechanisms and unreliable reasoning participants predates the current dedicated research programme. Reuse that programme rather than creating a parallel distributed-systems investigation.

## R24 — reviewer independence versus evidence completeness

A historical adversarial review episode reportedly yielded a decisive falsification from the reviewer with full context, while less-informed reviewers returned partial verdicts. This is an observation, not a controlled causal result. One-shot independence and evidence completeness are separate variables: a fresh reviewer can receive sufficient relevant source evidence without receiving the builder's conclusions or answer labels. Compare matched fresh-review arms with differing evidence availability, including valid defect detection, cross-document contradictions, false alarms, time and token cost. A separate session-history comparison would require its own controls. This complements, rather than duplicates, graph-versus-source representation and review-gate-value questions. The framework comparison should measure construction separately from authoring and deployment security/operational surfaces; the remote-execution question concerns independently revocable authority, not trusting a local model or recalling data already sent to a provider.
