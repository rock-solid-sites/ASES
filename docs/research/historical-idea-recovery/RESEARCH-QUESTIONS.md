Private source locators, unpublished portfolio ideas, benchmark answer material and original chats remain outside this public record.

## HIR-034 — classification evidence is preserved, not lost

The historical classification exercise survives byte-identically. The original batch at [b8680bd](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/tree/b8680bd0f3d5dc4bdd19e8fcd432c4087b3383e5), later filing at [ef24547](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/tree/ef24547caa8759f6c84e31009714359d66ddcbc4), and inspected main at [8db37e6](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/tree/8db37e68fe2c3a9e967f7da3edcf3a382ae44c06) were compared by exact blob identity. Eleven blobs (prompt and ten response/qualified records) match, with the originals retained under to-file/ on main. The later filing paths are not present on inspected main. A separate supplementary Qwen record also survives. Do not infer loss merely from absent filing paths.

Eight nominally attributed non-corrupt responses were compared at the thematic level; the remaining records are not counted as additional independent votes. Their proposed track counts range from four to seven (one four-track response explicitly allows three). They overlap on authority, evidence, coordination, evaluation and methodology but partition those concerns differently. The original prompt already specified the three levels and supplied examples; agreement with that framing is not independent discovery of a theorem. This comparison does not establish universal abstraction, exhaustive classification or model independence beyond recorded attribution.

**Unresolved question:** What distinction among research question, methodology prescription, and implementation observation predicts a materially different decision or reusable result? Compare actual cases where classifications would yield different artifacts, authority or experiment design. Preserve multi-axis classifications when a single hierarchy loses meaning. Evidence for the question belongs to the existing ontology and methodology investigations, not a new mandatory taxonomy.

## HIR-023 — two benchmark assets, not one

The earlier conceptual-review v0 benchmark and the later P7 benchmark are distinct six-case suites. P7's [pinned implementation and documents](https://github.com/rock-solid-sites/ASES/tree/f40da189237896a146ad5913ddbd1aca13e488ed) specify a newly constructed corpus; case identity was checked against the earlier source. Equal case counts and similar aims do not prove continuity of cases. No answer material is reproduced here.

P7 preparation includes a corrected conformance packet, but its independent model comparison remains NOT_RUN. It is inappropriate to treat preparation, deterministic checking, or repair of a packet as independent reviewer evidence. The historical v0 suite must also be evaluated separately if used.

**Unresolved question:** On distinct, frozen cases with independently derived scoring evidence, do reviewers discriminate valid conceptual structures from invalid ones, and can they explain the distinction without reproducing supplied framing? Do not substitute the preparation status of one benchmark for execution of either. Benchmark selection and any future execution need separate authorization.

## HIR-010 — distinguish missing artifacts from missing research

The older bounded-judgment/Jev inquiry contains surviving preparation history and reported later experimental results, including a reported length-confound qualification. The reported final measured artifacts and length review could not be retrieved through the inspected current repository/branch routes. A historical completion report is evidence that a result was reported, not independently inspected measurement data. The custody gap is not a reason to repeat the experiment by default, and the negative lookup is not proof of deletion.

**Unresolved question:** Recover exact measured artifacts, evaluator inputs, scoring procedure and comparison scope; establish whether the reported distinction between distributional results and stronger decision-policy claims survives inspection. Only then decide whether an additional control is necessary.

## Method efficacy — separate process compliance from outcomes

The [Phase 4 retrospective](../retrospectives/phases/Phase%204.md) preserves the original evaluation ambition, including safety and effective engineering for nonprogrammers, different model capabilities, speed and token expenditure. The [Evaluation Framework](../frameworks/Evaluation%20Framework.md) distinguishes implementation fidelity, methodology outcomes, research quality and experimental comparisons. The [AI Evaluation Protocol](../protocols/AI%20Evaluation%20Protocol.md) concerns capabilities and controlled comparison; it is not a substitute for a methodology-outcome study.

**Residual question:** on a bounded, comparable engineering task, does a specified methodological intervention improve externally verified outcomes, rework, elapsed time, model/resource expenditure, recovery and operator intervention? A component test establishes a component claim, not an overall methodology advantage; adherence to the process is not an independent success criterion. Reuse the existing evaluation framework and specific experimental work; no new permanent benchmark or mandatory gate follows from this recovery.

## Upstream-change awareness — test the decision, not the presence of a rule

The [upstream compatibility method](../../methodology/Software%20Customization%20and%20Upstream%20Compatibility%20Method.md) and [research addendum](../../proposals/2026-11-02-testing-debugging-ontology/upstream-change-awareness-addendum-2026-11-02.md) already preserve the relevant policy and proposed discriminating cases. The proposed original pilot comparisons were not established as executed in this pass. The useful residual is the effect of an upstream-first check on a genuinely fixed issue, an unfixed issue, and an apparently related change which does not fix the problem. Measure unnecessary work avoided, decision correctness and check overhead; a matching issue title or version bump is not proof. This is not a proposal for a new poller, duplicated compatibility workflow, or revival of a retired integration.
