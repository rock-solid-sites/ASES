# EDASES Review Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the methodology for reviewing EDASES artefacts, repositories and research outputs.

Its objective is to maximise the quality of review through independent reasoning, explicit evidence and structured synthesis rather than consensus alone.

---

# Principles

Reviews should be:

* independent
* evidence-driven
* reproducible
* traceable
* constructive

The objective of review is to improve the project rather than defend existing decisions.

---

# Review Process

A review proceeds through five stages.

1. **Orientation** — understand the project's purpose, terminology and architecture.
2. **Independent Analysis** — evaluate the assigned material without influence from other reviewers.
3. **Finding Generation** — record observations, findings and recommendations with supporting evidence.
4. **Synthesis** — compare independent reviews, identify agreement, disagreement and unresolved questions.
5. **Human Decision** — a human reviewer determines which findings should influence the project.

Consensus is evidence, not authority.

---

# Review Outputs

Every review should distinguish between:

* observations
* findings
* recommendations
* open questions

Each should remain traceable to supporting evidence.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Reviews should consider whether the repository:

* preserves abstraction boundaries
* maintains conceptual consistency
* avoids duplicated concepts
* preserves traceability
* follows the documentation standard
* aligns with canonical documents
* supports the stated project objectives

---

# Human Authority

Human reviewers retain ultimate responsibility for accepting, rejecting or deferring recommendations.

Review methodology supports decision-making; it does not replace it.

The outcome of the methodology is specifically considered the work of the human user and not the agent(s).
