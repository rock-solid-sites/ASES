## How to read credit value
Illustrative conversion for comparable **8 GB x86 shared-CPU VM**: at a hypothetical compute-plus-disk rate of **$0.10/hour**, $100 = 1,000 billable hours; at **$0.20/hour**, $100 = 500 hours. These are scenario assumptions, **not verified provider SKUs or quotes**. Always cap by credit expiry, provisioning/availability, SKU eligibility, concurrency, egress, disk, tax and individual credit terms. At $0.10–$0.20/h, a 30-day always-on VM costs ~$72–$144; 90 days ~$216–$432; 6 months ~$432–$864 (30-day months). A 12GB+ machine may cost appreciably more.
