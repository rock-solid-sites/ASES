## AD-001 — Handoff sufficiency is a consumer- and operation-relative predicate

**Evidence:** Historical operator-reported incident: an agent that had authored onboarding material asserted that the document and knowledge store were sufficient for a new session. A fresh session recovered approximately the intended vocabulary but recharacterized concepts, gathered information and failed before performing the requested action; a repetitive generation failure also occurred. The operator corrected the description of the underlying project and distinguished application maturity from documentation maturity. The transcript supports the reported discrepancy; the mechanism of the repetition, source admission completeness and causal explanation are unresolved. It is not a measured benchmark.

**Mechanism / question:** A reusable representation is sufficient for a particular authorized operation only if the consumer can reconstruct the relevant semantics, uncertainty, applicable constraints and state. A document can support description without supporting execution. The producer's confidence in a compressed representation is not validation of its use by a fresh consumer. Compare the cost of retaining relevant distinctions or targeted retrieval with the cost of reconstructing them, including erroneous reconstruction, redundant search, and intervention. A cheap discriminator should test operation-relevant fidelity, not merely repetition of vocabulary. Do not assume that a mandatory plan, full reread, or self-report of understanding establishes fidelity; these could themselves add redundant work.

**Domain:** agent cognition, coordination and execution. **Technique:** semantic sufficiency, lossy abstraction, representation validity, negative evidence. Current owner: [task-scoped information and handoff method](../../methodology/ASES%20Task-Scoped%20Information%20and%20Handoff%20Method.md) distinguishes effective task view, source admission, revision and evidence; [recurring coordination failures](../../../research/agent-information-topology/greenfield-task-context/field-trial/RECURRING-COORDINATION-FAILURES-2026-11-26.md) distinguishes observable failures from unresolved causes. This historical case is a candidate for that existing investigation, not proof of recurrence or a requirement for a new bootstrap artifact.

**Versus/Rust handoff corroboration:** The operator explicitly challenged whether the proposed specification was sufficient for a fresh-context builder. Successive review rounds then exposed undefined genesis, lifecycle, request grammar, rights/extents, event freshness and authorization choices that could force a conforming builder to stop or invent semantics. The final prompt records more precise pins; the source ends before a builder result. A specification can be reused to avoid reconstruction only after its load-bearing choices are sufficiently explicit for the intended operation. A reviewer's launch verdict is not a fresh-builder execution test. Do not promote historical pins into the current handoff method.

**Build-roadmap archive adequacy:** The operator first rejects a builder/reviewer brief that refers to an absent primitive definition; reviewers without repository access cannot resolve the reference. Later, the operator proposes testing existing end-of-session summaries before retrieving or re-extracting every raw conversation. These are complementary rather than contradictory: a sealed independent conformance task needs the complete normative semantic payload and accessible evidence, while an historical research query may reuse a compressed derivative if it preserves the specific observation, reasoning, uncertainty and revision distinctions needed. Locate missing claim-relative evidence before reopening the raw source; retain the source and do not assume that summary adequacy establishes complete topic coverage. The reported retrieval/tool limits and sample document audit are historical, not proof that compression or omission is safe for the present archive.

## AD-002 — Verification must terminate at the obligation, not at universal certainty

**Evidence:** In the same early discussion, an assistant proposed a reconstruction gate, adversarial reality check and evidence-backed execution, and the operator explicitly challenged the recursive regress of verifying verifiers. The operator then asked whether a narrower check could be automated on cheaper hardware, and questioned whether such tooling belonged inside the harness. Historical proposals included checking unsupported claims and assumption/decision traceability separately from generating the project model. No such checker was measured in this exchange; numerical self-confidence, supposed ease of verification and the donor capability descriptions are unverified.

**Mechanism / question:** Distinguish a decision's actual proof/evidence obligation from proving the entire consumer's understanding. Bound the validation by the requested operation, authority, observable acceptance and a finite stopping rule; accept residual uncertainty only where the relevant rule allows it. Reuse accepted scoped evidence rather than recursively revalidating its producer, subject to changed dependencies and trust/authority. Cheap mechanical reference/identity checks can precede semantic review, but a valid reference or unsupported self-reported confidence is not proof of entailment. A specialized reviewer may need less context than the generator; its correctness, false-accept rate, independence and total cost still require evidence. A finite budget alone does not justify execution on failure.

**Domain:** verification, agent cognition, orchestration. **Technique:** bounded obligation, selective validation, separation of generation/checking, proof/evidence custody. The [current task-scoped method](../../methodology/ASES%20Task-Scoped%20Information%20and%20Handoff%20Method.md) already selects the cheapest relevant falsifier, separates receiver roles, and specifies authority/acceptance. Historical confidence-score and mandatory bootstrap-stage proposals are not current governing procedure. A harness-independent contract/consumer split is a candidate relationship, not a new enforcement design.

## AD-003 — Reusable evidence and permission to act are distinct products

**Evidence:** Later exchanges explicitly asked whether enforcement should reside in the harness; the historical candidate separated project knowledge/state, validation, authority and execution, with a scoped expiring capability proposed as an interface. The closing retrospective treated this as a hypothesis to investigate, not implementation selection. It separately retained a reported generation/runtime anomaly, with no established cause. The available conversation includes truncated incident excerpts and the later assistant's account of retrieving fuller evidence; the underlying attachments are not independently recovered here.

**Mechanism / question:** If validity evidence can be produced once and consumed by different execution adapters, repeated reconstruction or policy interpretation might be avoidable. However, an evidence receipt is not itself permission: a grant depends on operation, actor, current authority, state/freshness and enforcement at the actual effect boundary. A valid result from one execution does not transfer a stale or foreign grant. Conversely, reconstructing all project knowledge inside each harness is not inherently necessary. Separate semantic proof obligations from runtime failure detection; a correct source selection or representation does not eliminate transport/decoder failures. A numerical confidence field cannot by itself establish authority or sufficient evidence.

**Domain:** governance, execution, verification. **Technique:** scoped evidence/authority contracts, compositional interfaces, portability and invalidation. Current owner: [task-scoped method](../../methodology/ASES%20Task-Scoped%20Information%20and%20Handoff%20Method.md) separates authority, evidence, source and consumer; current Work Unit/kernel research owns actual effect authority and enforcement. This is a historical precursor, not a claim that the proposed capability format or layer architecture is currently adopted, that a donor implements it, or that semantic checks can explain a runtime anomaly.

**Native composition counterexample:** The historical integration report describes F1 accepted content paired with stale observations after recovery, and F4 publishing an effect that the proposed F3 descendant mapping denied. Matching identities, hashes and locally valid transitions did not establish a common authority relationship. The follow-up reported an atomic subject-bound evidence repair for the changed-content path, retaining prior observations and testing unchanged reacceptance, but external freshness and global lifecycle authority remained distinct. This is a concrete reason to reuse the evidence only under its claim and subject, while obtaining current authority at the effect boundary. The archived PR #10/#11 results were not independently rerun in this pass.

## AD-004 — Reuse the decision structure, not just its outputs

**Evidence:** A historical continuation summary supplied by the operator and the subsequent assistant response explicitly distinguish static, deterministically derived, cached and newly synthesized decision structures. They propose narrowing a large command/action space into local answer sets, pruning impossible alternatives mechanically, applying bounded semantic judgment only to the unresolved alternatives, and requesting general reasoning when the structure is inadequate. The original conversation behind the supplied summary and external model/benchmark claims are not independently verified by this continuation. Historical role assignments are not current adopted architecture.

**Mechanism / question:** A decision structure itself is a computational artifact, not merely a sequence of cached answers. Reuse a validated structure across instances when its capability vocabulary, constraints, dependency state and semantics remain applicable; derive a new one mechanically when possible, repair/synthesize only where derivation and reuse fail. The smaller per-node input and local action set could avoid repeated search over the global action space. Counterexamples: omitted legal alternatives, stale capabilities, observations insufficient for a local choice, or interacting branches that make locally valid choices globally unsound. A bounded answer distribution is neither a deterministic fact nor permission to execute. Context reduction and calibration need to be evaluated against equivalent outcome/authority obligations. Cost of construction, invalidation, pruning, validation, escalation and execution must all be counted.

**Domain:** decision support, workflow execution, agent cognition. **Technique:** partial evaluation, reusable intermediate representation, decomposition, deterministic pruning, bounded probabilistic judgment and escalation. This extends the existing System-1 research lead in [SOURCE-MAP](SOURCE-MAP.md) without selecting a provider, requiring a new subsystem, or treating the decision structure as canonical by default. Whether it is a derived optimization or the primary work representation remains open.

## AD-005 — Memory reuse can retain what happened without retaining why it matters

**Evidence:** A separate short historical survey explicitly accumulates sources on selective remembering/forgetting, replay vs compaction and learned retrieval into active context. The user supplies third-party claims about performance/compaction; the assistant distinguishes event, causal history and present relevance, and agent memory from durable work state. No performance claim or learned retriever was tested in this conversation. The historical [memory research retrospective](../retrospectives/topics/EDASES-topic-Memory-Research.md) already separates compact normalized experience, immutable evidence and latent computation state; preserve that ownership.

**Mechanism / question:** For a task, retrieval must preserve counterfactual/causal distinctions such as why an approach failed, which scope was tested, and what constraints invalidate earlier results; a summary that preserves outcomes may omit exactly the discriminator needed to avoid repeating work. A candidate learned retrieval policy may select a minimal relevant view rather than replay everything; retention and selection have different obligations. Keep source evidence reconstructable, the derived selection fallible, and authoritative work state separate. Assess saved inference and retrieval work together with omissions, stale selections, and cost of recovery. Selective forgetting is not permission to discard required evidence.

**Domain:** agent memory, archive/retrieval, durable knowledge. **Technique:** demand-driven context selection, lossy representation, evidence provenance and learned retrieval. Related to AD-001 and existing task-scoped information and Knowledge Index research; no new memory product choice or benchmark.

**Earlier corroboration:** The UI-design discussion explicitly distinguishes preserving every historical state from retaining the rationale for replacement or removal. A versioned current plan, its rejected predecessor, the assumption that failed and the supporting test can provide a bounded reconstruction path for a fresh agent without replaying the conversation. Original sessions remain discoverable for questions the projection cannot answer. This is a hypothesis about context reconstruction, not proof that a fixed version graph is sufficient, that deletion is safe, or that one particular storage model should be adopted. Human operational views and agent reasoning views may expose different projections of the same evidence; retain source access and measure recovery as well as token savings.

**Foundational corroboration:** The knowledge-architecture charter went through two review rounds. The first response omitted one review; the narrowly framed charter also prompted objections that partly depended on missing motivating experience. Subsequent revisions made actual failure modes, cross-project reuse, evaluation questions and the proposed common corpus explicit. The later archive-package exchange is a concrete loss-of-fidelity incident: a condensed derivative was offered where the operator wanted exact preservation, and the assistant could no longer guarantee verbatim reconstruction. A source-preserving archive and a navigation/analysis projection serve different purposes. A cheap index must be evaluated against actual retrieval and reasoning-reconstruction tasks, including revision, supersession and disagreement; maintaining richly typed relationships is itself a cost. The reported creation of simple provisional repository directories establishes a structural change, not demonstrated recovery benefit or a final knowledge ontology. The later retrospective is derivative evidence, not an independent observation.

## AD-006 — Avoid idle residency without reusing obsolete authority

**Evidence:** A historical comparison of agent execution substrates proposes separating durable work identity from a disposable execution instance, reclaiming idle physical workers through suspension/restoration, and reusing an environment declaration across executions. These are architectural proposals; no resource measurements or donor conformance tests were performed in the conversation.

**Mechanism / question:** Persistent work state need not imply persistent reservation of the compute that performs the work. A reusable environment specification need not imply reuse of its materialized environment. The optimization is only sound where the restored state, external dependencies and execution authority remain valid; snapshot capture, storage, restoration, reconciliation and fresh authorization costs must be included. Avoiding idle residency is distinct from avoiding recomputation on resume. An authentic saved state may be obsolete, and restarting or restoring an execution must not revive its former authority.

**Domain:** execution substrate, scheduling, persistence. **Technique:** temporal decomposition, demand-driven materialization, checkpoint/restoration, resource release. Current owner: [tenure reuse and delayed admission](../kernel-0/Tenure-reuse-and-delayed-admission.md) establishes a scoped stale-write counterexample and conditions for safe identifier reuse under explicit premises. It constrains the candidate optimization without demonstrating implementation conformance or recommending a snapshot mechanism. No change to kernel primitives or Work Unit architecture is proposed.

**Corroboration from earlier environment research:** Separate reproducible environment definition, realized dependency/image identity, isolation substrate and mutable execution instance. The early container/microVM/Nix discussions explicitly propose an addressable environment specification as evidence provenance and as a way to avoid agents repeating imperative setup. A specification is not a certificate of the realized environment; lockfiles, architecture, source inputs, caches, nondeterministic builds and external dependencies may change the claim-relevant conditions. Reuse an already-realized immutable dependency closure or cached image only when it satisfies the new execution's required properties; still admit authority afresh and validate isolation. A mutable resumed workspace is a different reuse object from a reproducible declaration or immutable base. The early technology comparisons and lifecycle sketches were proposals, not reproducibility or security experiments. No Nix, container or microVM dependency is adopted.

**Historical celld/state refinement:** The operator explicitly separated durable work from a per-work database, then challenged a Git-only representation when relational queries and mandatory metadata became necessary. Proposed recovery experiments independently remove worker, context, worktree, machine and harness, and add metadata only when a missing concept causes a measurable recovery failure. Logical work, durable recovery evidence, materialized execution and current authority are separate identities; worktree removal is not itself proof of a recoverable checkpoint or isolation. Compare recovered correctness and repeated rediscovery costs, not just ability to restart. The early Git/SQLite/celld/Temporal alternatives are historical hypotheses, not storage choices or a current definition of execution identity.

**Demand-activated resource presentation:** In the historical deployment-platform discussion the operator rejects a continuously running management stack for the required subset of capabilities, and specifies that merely opening a dashboard page must not start collection; activation is explicit and final session loss should stop dashboard-owned activity. The proposed session lease/disconnection mechanism addresses the fact that browser departure notification is not guaranteed. A static shell, existing ingress and on-demand process are distinct from zero total resident cost. Separate optional presentation and sampling from indispensable ongoing accounting, independent limit enforcement, and retained event history. The reported host reconnaissance found insufficient disk headroom and unverified private ingress, so activation/termination and performance remained untested. Compare startup and shutdown, concurrent viewers, orphaned descendants, measurement freshness and idle versus active cost; no specific heartbeat, hosting, process mechanism or dashboard deployment is adopted.

**Recovery and independent computation:** The operator distinguishes already-granted internal computation from access to revocable external effects after an engine failure; historical attachments are candidate inputs for reassessment, not restored grants. This helps separate recomputation, continued execution, historical observation and reauthorization as different recovery obligations. The earlier optional-quiescence profile and attachment terminology are not current Work Unit requirements. The source reports an architectural reconciliation, not independently established realized enforcement.

**Persistent access state versus idle compute:** The plugin survey explicitly corrects a persistent-host proposal into on-demand execution where supported authorization state can survive the worker's absence. Restorable local state, usable current authorization, a supported capture mechanism and accessible source identity are separate obligations. Historical browser/runtime proposals are not validated authentication or current platform permission. A reported managed-browser page-load failure and successful infrastructure probes distinguish the relevant capability from deployment health. Compare startup and retained-state maintenance against idle resource use, not speculative continuously running capacity.

**T3-to-Paseo runtime evidence:** A historical audit reported roughly 247 MB retained per distinct completed workspace, no additional MCP processes on reuse of an existing workspace, and no MCP release when a completed agent was deleted; a later native-Codex compatibility trial reported all dedicated processes released after archival. The relevant distinction is between session completion, owner lifetime, evidence capture, archival and observed process exit. Process reuse must preserve concurrent consumers and their authority boundaries; a smaller process group or unconditional removal is not sufficient evidence. These are archived measurements and not a current-system claim.

**Disposable guest versus durable obligations.** The VPS value survey distinguishes reconstructible OS/toolchains and caches from irreplaceable uncommitted work, evidence and credential recovery. Guest rebuild, independent console recovery, provider replacement, and revocation of exposed authority are different obligations; backups sharing the failed account or provider cannot establish independent recovery. The operator rejects whole-machine backup as a requirement where the unique information can be retained. Host capacity should be measured against the remaining active services and their peaks, not against historically colocated workers. These are historical requirements and proposals, not verified recovery tests or a hosting choice.

## AD-007 — Reuse an experiment's discriminating behavior rather than its production infrastructure

**Evidence:** A historical database comparison separates lightweight local transactional storage from distributed consistency and fault-tolerance, and proposes importing deterministic failure simulation rather than the database itself. The proposed example involves executor disappearance, retry and late completion. This is a candidate research direction, not a verified donor result or a new database choice. The historical user identifies lightweight sufficient storage as the default; the technical claims about specific database versions and deployment requirements were not independently rechecked.

**Mechanism / question:** Represent the observable correctness obligation and failure schedule in a smaller model before constructing the full distributed system. A reproducible schedule or minimal counterexample can be reusable across implementations if the same relevant transitions, fault assumptions and observation points are represented. Avoiding production-scale execution for each reasoning or verification iteration is different from substituting simulation for conformance evidence. A model omitting delayed completion, lost responses, independent ownership or an external effect may prove the wrong claim. Replay by seed alone also depends on the simulator and its version. The current [tenure-reuse experiment](../kernel-0/Tenure-reuse-and-delayed-admission.md) provides an explicit scoped example of a bounded model and its exclusions; retain that ownership.

**Domain:** storage, distributed execution, assurance. **Technique:** abstraction, deterministic simulation, counterexample reuse, bounded checking. This extends the existing testing and verification area without treating simulation, a donor technology, or its architecture as adopted.

## AD-008 — Necessity testing can eliminate a whole computation rather than optimize it

**Evidence:** An operator-reported case describes two agents planning an elaborate archive ingestion pipeline when direct native file upload worked with negligible reported delay. The operator proposed a deliberately sparse adversarial existence review, explicitly permitted failure to establish the negative claim, and applied the same necessity question recursively to the review machinery itself. A subsequent research brief proposes a controlled comparison of objective-only versus search-path-prescribing prompts, including unanticipated defects and clean controls. This is a reported case and a proposed study, not measured prompt superiority, benchmark completion or independent verification.

**Mechanism / question:** Before optimizing a proposed transformation, test whether an existing operation already satisfies its acceptance condition, or whether the transformation is needed at all. A cheap discriminating observation may collapse the entire planned computation. The same test applies to the verification overhead, not only the implementation. Avoiding predetermined search paths may permit discovery of a cheaper adequate operation outside the proposed design; conversely, a sparse prompt cannot replace required authority, scope, evidence or acceptance conditions. Failure to find an objection is not proof of necessity. Preserve scope: one successful upload does not establish suitability for every archive size, format, repeated query or privacy constraint.

**Domain:** research, orchestration, tool selection, verification. **Technique:** demand elimination, early cutoff, falsification, selection cost, metalevel verification cost. The [current compact-prompt skill](../../../skills/current/compact-prompt/SKILL.md) prefers task-relevant information and avoids implementation anchors; [review-launch](../../../skills/current/review-launch/SKILL.md) distinguishes exploratory criticism from frozen-claim verification. Do not create or dispatch a study here. Historical prompt research ownership and later evidence must be reconciled separately.

**Corroboration from the early Agent Communication discussion:** A proposed symmetric messaging protocol was reduced by the operator to an asymmetric composition of existing durable coordination records, an existing Observer notification envelope, and an existing downward prompt-delivery path. A minimal blocking/nonblocking declaration and incremental watcher were proposed; direct notification and schema changes were deferred until evidence justified them. This removes a whole proposed subsystem only if the existing paths meet the actual obligations: correct destination and execution identity, durable discoverability, duplicate handling, restart recovery, and delivery/acknowledgment semantics. A recorded question or self-declared blocking flag is not by itself an authoritative execution transition, evidence of prompt consumption, or license to suppress all liveness checks. A notification shortcut must not create a second truth or lose the durable record. This historical brief specifies proposed tests, not demonstrated exactly-once delivery or an implementation result; no obsolete coordination backend is reinstated.

**Upstream-solution avoidance:** The operator reports investigating a defect already fixed in upstream source before the local investigation started, and requests frequent upstream change checks. The historical CodeGraph incident illustrates the distinct propositions that a relevant fix exists, is included in a release, and resolves the exact local reproduction under the applicable configuration. An appropriately scoped version/issue/commit check can eliminate duplicate diagnosis or patch construction; do not treat a related upstream issue or release title as validation. Recheck when a material premise changes or before an expensive irreversible action, rather than continuously resurveying without demand. The archived addendum and claimed past binding instruction are not current policy authority.

**Requirement narrowing after host loss:** When an existing execution host became inaccessible, the initial proposal rebuilt a coordination bridge on a replacement host. The operator clarified that the immediate obligation was only issue tracking; an existing hosted tracker could already perform it without restoring the lost CLI, SQLite state or execution features. Later the same question was applied to the interactive agent frontend: the intended strategy/delegation/evidence-return cycle need not depend on a resident daemon when a direct bounded dispatch route can satisfy it. The actual launch routes were not then validated. Distinguish requirement elimination from migration, and loss of unpushed state from replacement of an accessible service. Current project tracker and launch policies govern, not the historical alternatives.

## AD-009 — Replace repeated discretionary choices with small, scoped predicates

**Evidence:** In a separate historical policy-distribution discussion, the operator asked whether numerous coordination failures could collapse into a few simple decision gates, with ambiguous edge cases referred for decision. The proposed gates distinguished corresponding active work identity, current state versus enduring knowledge, general versus local guidance, and duplicate authority. The operator corrected an over-decomposed implementation plan because the bridge was intentionally temporary; recovery stubs remained necessary as linked recovery records rather than second trackers. A pasted audit and reported differences between harness enforcement are historical evidence, not independently verified current behavior.

**Mechanism / question:** A stable invariant can replace repeatedly reasoning about a large set of allowed cases. A mechanical predicate must bind the actual target and current state: merely having any active issue is weaker than having the corresponding active issue, and a check can become stale before the effect. Regular cases may take a cheap deterministic path; ambiguity or changed dependencies must escape it. Repeated ambiguous cases may justify a new rule only if their underlying semantics match. Reduce duplicated mutable facts, not distinct information obligations; a recovery artifact can remain necessary even when its work identity is already stored. Account for predicate design, maintenance, exceptions, escalation, enforcement and race conditions. This is an application of AD-004 and AD-003, not an assertion that all governance can be reduced to binary gates.

**Domain:** governance, information routing, task execution. **Technique:** predicate compilation, decision simplification, single-source authority, scope-aware escalation. Current [task-scoped method](../../methodology/ASES%20Task-Scoped%20Information%20and%20Handoff%20Method.md) defines information ownership and local adaptation review; [tenure-reuse research](../kernel-0/Tenure-reuse-and-delayed-admission.md) constrains stale authorization. The temporary Crosslink bridge and its historical file layout are superseded context, not instructions to restore them.

**Historical incident grounding:** The operator supplied contemporary agent reports of excessive reasoning loops without commits, missed checkpoint discipline despite existing knowledge, monitoring the wrong activity signal, and claimed relaunch without execution. The same operator corrected an automatic-timeout remedy using earlier cases of productive workers killed by nominal limits. A known rule being available or retrieved does not establish that it is applied, and eliminating manual reconstruction is distinct from delegating judgment. A mechanically established attention condition should not be conflated with the authority or evidence required for intervention. These reports motivate discriminating tests of productive long runs versus actual stalls and claimed versus observed recovery; no incident was independently replayed here, and historical timeout/cadence examples are not new policy.

## AD-010 — Representation equality does not establish admission or semantic agreement

**Evidence:** The same policy-distribution exchange challenged generating and committing a duplicate instruction document, and identified a lifecycle ordering risk: a consumer may already have read stale instructions before synchronization runs. The discussion distinguished canonical bytes, generated metadata, independent repository-specific guidance, and effective instructions. A temporary exact-copy bridge was proposed under a short lifetime; the later current method deliberately treats adapted consuming documents as reconciliation candidates, not automatic replacements.

**Mechanism / question:** Hash equality can cheaply establish identity of a defined byte payload, not that a receiver consumed it at the right time, selected the correct source, interpreted it correctly, or applied compatible local exceptions. Cheap validity checks should be staged by the obligation they discharge: identity, freshness, admission, semantic compatibility and actual enforcement. A synchronized copy can save retrieval at runtime while imposing update fanout, stale-copy and storage costs. Reference-based reuse avoids redundant copies only when the receiver can resolve the source reliably and within the required lifecycle. A generated derivative with its own mutable authority can increase work rather than reduce it.

**Domain:** shared knowledge, documentation, policy and execution. **Technique:** content identity, dependency and admission order, derived views, invalidation, compatibility. Current owner: [task-scoped information method](../../methodology/ASES%20Task-Scoped%20Information%20and%20Handoff%20Method.md), including local adaptation reconciliation. Historical bridge proposals are not current architectural authority; no new synchronization or schema is proposed.

## AD-011 — Amortized learned computation is not the same claim as reproducing a reasoning process

**Evidence:** A historical HyperThink discussion considered input-conditioned temporary parameter updates to a frozen model instead of producing a long explicit reasoning trace. The proposed discriminating controls compare native non-thinking/full-thinking/adapted outputs, matched versus swapped updates between structurally similar instances, held-out counterfactuals and teacher-error/correction behavior. It also distinguished training a separate update predictor from ordinary task-specific LoRA and proposed remote training/local inference. No reproduction was run; quoted paper metrics, implementation details and resource assumptions were not independently reverified in this archive pass.

**Mechanism / question:** Upfront training may amortize repeated expensive inference across many later instances, and a per-input update may substitute a different computation for token-by-token generation. Distinguish behavioral equivalence on a stated distribution, instance-specific adaptation, and mechanistic equivalence of intermediate computation. An answer match does not establish the last; a teacher's error may be reproduced as well. Evaluate the training, data generation, update generation, inference, validation and distribution-shift costs together; savings are conditional on sufficient use and adequate accuracy. The proposed matched/swapped-update discriminator illustrates how to test specificity of a derived computational artifact without claiming proof from mere output agreement. This is approximate learned substitution, not exact proof or cached-result reuse.

**Domain:** inference, model specialization, local/remote resource use. **Technique:** amortized inference, conditional parameterization, distillation, counterfactual controls. Related to the model/inference research in [SOURCE-MAP](SOURCE-MAP.md); the archived paper is a literature lead for later planning, not a selected experiment or provider.

## AD-012 — Retrieval recall is not the utility of the retrieved representation

**Evidence:** A historical embedding discussion proposed retaining raw evidence, normalized text and keyword retrieval, adding embeddings only as discovery aids. The operator explicitly preferred surveying alternatives rather than selecting one model and proposed a fresh chat without project memory to test retrieval and understanding. The assistant proposed fixed-context comparisons among no-retrieval, keyword, semantic and hybrid retrieval, including historical reasons, misleading near-matches and unanswerable questions. It also recorded a donor claim about compatible embeddings across modalities and variable-dimensional representations; neither compatibility nor reported benchmark quality was independently checked here. No ASES retrieval experiment was run in the conversation.

**Mechanism / question:** Reusable indexes may avoid repeated scanning and supply a smaller view to a consumer, but retrieval success must be separated from downstream correctness, source authority and interpretation (AD-001). Reuse of an index across dimensions, modalities or versions depends on compatibility of actual embedding functions and index/query contracts; similar vectors from unrelated models are not generally interchangeable. If compatibility holds, extending a representation without rebuilding earlier components is a distinct incremental-computation mechanism. Compressed dimensions may save storage and search cost at a fidelity tradeoff; test the intended decision rather than accepting a general retrieval score. Compare context budgets and total build/update/query/verification cost; include negative and superseded knowledge and allow abstention. A vector index alone does not deliver usable context.

**Domain:** repository analysis, knowledge retrieval, agent cognition. **Technique:** derived indexing, approximate search, representation compatibility, selective retrieval, end-to-end evaluation. Related to the existing [Knowledge Index prototype](../../../scripts/knowledge_index/README.md), AD-005, and [graph-assisted review rubric](GRAPH-ASSISTED-REVIEW.md); do not conflate structural graph retrieval with embeddings or infer adoption. No model selection, indexing of private data with external services, or new experiment is authorized.

**Early structural-retrieval refinement:** Repository analysis may describe an older disk or Git state instead of a dirty editor buffer, and mechanically derived results may cover only one feature/configuration or partial macro resolution. Report those limitations and provenance rather than universal confidence. A query can save an initial file read yet create a costly series of follow-up lookups, stale-result repair or final raw-source fallback (the review calls this semantic ping-pong). Compare total end-to-end work, accuracy and actual useful substitution; reuse mature analyzers only against the smallest useful query contract, rather than building a second cache or exposing every analyzer capability. The reported review and engineering spikes are proposals, not measured performance.

**Crosslink corpus refinement:** A historical collection task separates cheap broad acquisition and immutable raw retention from expensive thread hydration, contextual selection and interpretation. The operator eventually questions whether the collection was already overkill for the narrow historical question. The reported reduced corpus retained nonmatches for audit and sampled them for recall; even a substantial filtered corpus missed explicit architecture rationale later recovered via a known retrospective. This supplies a concrete reason to stop broad acquisition when the remaining question can be answered by a targeted primary source. Compare source coverage, missed-context risk, acquisition/retention costs and future reuse, rather than treating more collected records or lexical matches as research progress. Reported corpus counts, reduction results and raw attachments were not independently reopened.

**Repository-research evidence, not structural recall alone.** EDASES cgrmcp Plugin 1 reports initial failures of broad repository reading and a later paired CodeGraph/GitNexus benchmark. The reported design held pinned source, question and answer model constant, used fresh contexts, froze questions, and sought independent evaluation against source; 8/8 Helium/Crosslink verdicts favored CodeGraph, T3 split 2–2, and GitNexus incurred interface errors in five of six arms. Coverage differed materially, including CodeGraph's 65/563 Helium files; this is not evidence of universal superiority, and verdicts over shared answers are not independent trials. A no-indexable-files live smoke test later required a second code repository to establish structural exploration. Preserve explicit coverage and uncertainty, and distinguish deterministic structural facts, inferred semantic conclusions, and bounded raw-file evidence.

## AD-013 — Inference avoidance changes the economically useful granularity of the residual inference

**Evidence:** A historical promotional-application discussion contains an operator-authored clarification: eliminate inference wherever deterministic state, verification and reuse suffice; faster remaining inference makes finer-grained feedback practical. Later turns explicitly ask whether sophisticated expert processes reduce to classification, whether frontier reasoning is principally needed for novel conceptual structures or high-consequence cases, and whether simulation can be a bounded computational operation. The conversation is a design hypothesis and statement of research intent, not measured latency, throughput or adopted component architecture.

**Mechanism / question:** Decompose a task into known structure, deterministic transitions, bounded semantic choices, and unresolved structure creation. The need for higher reasoning is not necessarily proportional to apparent task difficulty; it may depend on whether a suitable representation already exists. Reducing call count, context size and inference latency are separate optimization axes.
