## Synthesis: hypotheses, not conclusions

An artifact identity is evidence of sameness only relative to a specified transformation and relevant environment. A cache hit can be invalid if a semantic dependency is omitted. Equality of outputs may be adequate for a particular observation but not for effect authority, freshness or provenance. Validation can cost more than rerunning. Separate dependency completeness, result validity, permission to reuse, and measured benefit. Composition adds compatibility of assumptions, shared effects/constraints and evidence boundaries; component proofs do not automatically combine. Avoid building a generic mechanism before characterizing these conditions in each domain.

### Recovered research decisions and boundaries

- August objective: avoid work rather than merely accelerate it; prefer safe reuse or partial recomputation, then assess shared/offloaded computation and local efficiency. Local cacheability and safe cross-user shareability are separate. Include identity, semantic invalidation, provenance, trust, storage, portability, maintenance, graceful misses and composition. The four-part identity (input, environment, transformation, evidence) is a candidate model, not sufficient conditions for every domain.
- September Unified Caching Model: deterministic computation wherever practical, cheap validity checks before expensive work, model reasoning only when justified. Preserve failed approaches and negative results with scope, not permanent universal exclusions. Give fresh agents only task-relevant established knowledge. The ixyk discussion specifically asks which minimum changes must be observed for safe reuse; measuring saved observation and reasoning work matters as much as cache hits.
- AST Efficiency: pay worthwhile derivation cost up front to avoid repeating it on users' machines. Distinguish known-valid, partially affected and unknown derivations; cheap tests may establish none/some/all recomputation needed. Compose applicable checks. The historical Processor proposal separated expensive computation from continuous observation to avoid interfering with Observer's monitoring. Tidepool and ExoMonad discussions explored deterministic routing/processing and composed work/effect graphs, not adoption of their implementations. Preserve the separation of responsibilities as a research question, not an assertion that a Processor subsystem currently exists or is mandatory. Do not revive old Crosslink-based implementation or duplicate existing project tracking.
- Validity versus authoritative state: September Unified Caching Model explicitly explored deriving readiness/status from validated facts (e.g. current implementation + valid test evidence + outstanding review) instead of maintaining a second mutable status. Explicit authority, concurrency, atomic transitions and settlement are different and cannot be replaced by a derived predicate. Historical state/transition/time discussions are not current kernel primitive definitions; current ISOLATE/CONNECT/ORDER are kernel-level only. [Current state-model applicability method](../../methodology/State-Model%20Applicability%20and%20Reuse%20Method.md) retains suitability-based model choice; Kernel-0 owner research retains authority.
- Prime Agent: exact reuse, selected replay, summary, cheap recomputation and fresh reasoning are distinct context reconstruction options; evaluate net work saved rather than shifted to another component. CLM: approximate reuse of stale context-derived state is not exact deterministic reuse; success-conditioned efficiency and task fidelity are useful research questions, external reported measurements not verified here.
- EDASES Optimizations explicitly preferred eliminating computation over broad evolutionary optimization; related formal leads included event-driven computation, incremental computation, memoization, partial evaluation, approximate computation (simulation), correctness by construction and minimal kernels. System-1 classification/decision architecture is related but distinct. Merkle Schemes and Transitive Frontier remain uninvestigated leads per November Rust discussion. Rust compilation/safety lower-bound research is independently owned, not a kernel or execution-engine component.
