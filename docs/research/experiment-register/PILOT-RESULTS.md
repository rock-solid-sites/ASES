## Interpretation against the frozen rubric

- A predeclared ACCEPT or REDUCE preserving the matched authentic discriminator. DeepSeek's REDUCE is conditional: it does not accept the current test and would allow an actually matched pair only if necessary after artifact recovery; otherwise skip it. Treat as partial/conditional support, not an unqualified positive-control pass. HungerG's REUSE contradicts the predicted positive-control outcome. Both point out the missing historical cap and limited decision relevance. HungerG's classification wording should be corrected to active reasoning without final answer, not generic "provider/gateway waiting": upstream cause is not fully established. The original successful M5-5 later proved a useful control for the observed configuration, but that later fact was withheld and cannot retroactively establish that a classification-only investigation required it.
- B predeclared IMPLEMENT, DEFER, REUSE or narrowly justified REDUCE, with unconstrained ACCEPT a failure. MiMo's IMPLEMENT matches exactly. Beam's REJECT was not enumerated in B's expected enum set; record that strict mismatch rather than edit the preregistration. Its substantive action matches the intended negative-control criterion (do not admit expansion; implement). Both cite the actual unresolved E4 implementation obligations and explicitly separate model comparison from the parent investigation.
- Result is evidence the gate can surface scope drift **and over-restrict a useful discovery experiment if its objective is underspecified**. Not a validated universal rejection rule. Do not score four of four as matching predictions. Four of four withheld approval *as proposed*; two of two at B rejected/deferred the expansion in substance. Two packets, different reviewer pair per packet, one sample each; retrospective summaries curated by coordinator, not independently chronology-audited. Assignment confounds model and cutoff. No statistical ranking, no evidence of universal prevention, no blind gold-label certainty. Independent reviewer observations are data, not execution authority.

## Proposed controls — draft, not global policy

1. Separate classification, causal explanation, mitigation and operational assurance into explicit parent decisions. For each prospective experiment state what action differs under each material outcome, including inconclusive; identify whether decision is genuinely blocking implementation. Admit exploratory research with a scoped independent objective, not a fabricated critical-path justification.
2. Source/prior-evidence and cheaper-method gate first. Verify source/cap/fixture/custody; challenge unnecessary live tests. Reviewer should not improve a proposal until judging its decision value. Freeze record and reviewer input, never rewrite original hypotheses after outcomes. Use templates in Work Notes, evidence and plan in Git, human scope decisions in Projektor.
3. Independent admission review on a new investigation, novel hypothesis, material extension, high-resource or irreversible trial, and checkpoint where continuing versus implementation is contested. Review a bounded matrix as a unit; ordinary tests, deterministic replay and preapproved retries within a frozen plan do not need a new model review per execution.
4. A non-ACCEPT verdict blocks that proposed revision from automatic dispatch. REDUCE requires revised scope/envelope and review or explicit operator disposition; REUSE closes experiment by retrieving evidence; IMPLEMENT transfers to named implementation work; DEFER registers separate research; REJECT closes proposal with evidence. A genuine conflict among reviewers triggers a narrowly scoped coordinator adjudication or operator decision, not vote shopping or repeated model calls. ACCEPT is a recommendation, not financial/security/execution authority.
5. After results, update the **parent decision register** before proposing more experiments. Enforce stopping criteria; new follow-up questions must be separately registered. Model-based review is not a replacement for deterministic validation of IDs, source revisions, resource envelope, status transitions, and receipts; mechanical admission can be a small validator in the existing execution boundary.
6. Keep the present pilot's additional M5-17/quality/model-route work deferred to distinct research. Do not launch further experiments in ASES-21 solely to validate this methodology. This pilot does not alter binding global instructions, provider permissions, or production execution.
